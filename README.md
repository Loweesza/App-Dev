#  Crazy Rich Asians

## Overview
**Crazy Rich Asians** is a *romantic comedy* film based on the novel by Kevin Kwan.  
It follows Rachel Chu, an economics professor, who discovers that her boyfriend Nick Young is from one of the wealthiest families in Singapore.

---

###  Main Characters
1. **Rachel Chu** – A smart and kind-hearted woman from New York  
2. **Nick Young** – Rachel’s boyfriend and heir to a massive fortune  
3. **Eleanor Young** – Nick’s strict but elegant mother  
4. **Peik Lin Goh** – Rachel’s hilarious best friend  

---

###  Why I Love This Movie
- Beautiful representation of Asian culture and traditions  
- Stunning visuals and luxury fashion  
- *Heartwarming story about love, family, and identity*  
- Powerful message: **Love is worth fighting for**

---

### Favorite Quote
> “Eleanor had a long-held theory about men. She truly believed that for most men, all that talk of “being in love” or “finding the right one” was absolute nonsense.”

---

###  Fun Fact
`Crazy Rich Asians` was the first major Hollywood film in 25 years to feature an all-Asian cast.
